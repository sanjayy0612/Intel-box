# MongoDB Schema

1. `company_profiles`: business model, scale, positioning, perception, freshness metadata.
2. `competitor_map`: 3-5 competitor records with strengths, gaps, and activity.
3. `brand_activity`: campaigns, launches, PR, and event footprint.
4. `decision_makers`: stakeholder records plus role relevance.
5. `contact_intelligence`: email, LinkedIn, phone, source, confidence score.
6. `outreach_drafts`: versioned LinkedIn and email drafts.
7. `outreach_tracker`: channel status, sent/opened/replied timestamps, and follow-up due date.
